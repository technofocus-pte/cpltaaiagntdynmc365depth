# Detailed Procedure: Error Code E15

Symptoms: Brewing stops, the display shows E15, and no coffee is
dispensed.

## Possible Causes

- Water reservoir empty or incorrectly installed.

- Blocked water inlet.

- Scale buildup in the internal water circuit.

- Brew unit incorrectly fitted.

- Pump unable to maintain water flow.

## Resolution Steps

1.  Fill and reseat the water tank.

2.  Power off the machine for 30 seconds and restart.

3.  Run the automatic rinse cycle.

4.  Run the descaling program.

5.  Remove and reinstall the brew unit.

6.  If E15 persists after two attempts, contact Contoso Technical
    Support.

# Escalation Criteria

- E15 remains after completing all troubleshooting.

- Machine leaks water.

- Electrical or burning smell is detected.

- Pump or grinder makes abnormal noises.
