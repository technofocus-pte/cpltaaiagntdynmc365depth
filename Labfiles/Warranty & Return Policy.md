**CONTOSO ELECTRONICS**

**Warranty & Returns Policy**

*Smart Locks · Smart Thermostats · Smart Plugs*

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Document Purpose**

This policy is the authoritative knowledge source for answering customer
questions about warranty coverage, returns, and replacements for Contoso
Electronics smart-home devices. It is intended for use by customer
support agents and by the Contoso Electronics Support Agent (an AI agent
built in Microsoft Copilot Studio).

*Version 2.4 — Last updated July 2026*

The warranty period begins on the original purchase date as shown on a
valid receipt or the order confirmation on file with Contoso
Electronics. Products purchased through unauthorized third-party
resellers are not eligible for warranty service.

**2. What Is Covered**

The standard warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under
normal residential use, including:

- Hardware component failure (motors, sensors, Wi-Fi/BLE radio modules,
  relays).

- Manufacturing defects present at the time of purchase.

- Firmware-related malfunctions that cannot be resolved through a
  standard firmware update or factory reset.

- Premature battery drain caused by a device fault (not normal battery
  consumption).

**3. What Is Not Covered**

The following are excluded from warranty coverage under all Contoso
Electronics smart-home warranties:

- Physical damage from drops, impact, fire, flood, or exposure to
  extreme temperatures outside the published operating range (32°F–104°F
  / 0°C–40°C).

- Damage from unauthorized modification, disassembly, or third-party
  firmware.

- Normal wear items such as batteries, mounting adhesives, and
  faceplates after the 90-day accessory window.

- Cosmetic wear (scratches, discoloration) that does not affect
  function.

- Issues caused by the customer's home network, router configuration, or
  third-party smart-home platforms (e.g., third-party voice assistant
  integrations).

- Devices with the serial number removed, defaced, or illegible.

- Devices purchased used, refurbished by a third party, or through
  unauthorized resellers.

**4. Filing a Warranty Claim**

Support agents (human or AI) should guide customers through the
following steps when a hardware defect is suspected and troubleshooting
from the Smart Device Troubleshooting Guide has not resolved the issue:

1.  Confirm the device is within its warranty window using the purchase
    date and Warranty Coverage Summary table in Section 1.

2.  Confirm the issue is not on the exclusions list in Section 3.

3.  Collect the device model number and serial number (found on the
    device label or in the Contoso Home app under Device \> Device
    Info).

4.  Collect proof of purchase — order number, receipt, or the account
    email used at purchase.

5.  If all criteria are met, the case should be escalated to a live
    specialist to issue a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number.
    The AI support agent does not issue RMA numbers directly.

Claims cannot be processed without a valid RMA number obtained from a
live specialist or the Contoso Electronics support portal.

**5. Returns Policy (Non-Warranty)**

**5.1 Standard Return Window**

Unopened or like-new devices may be returned for a full refund within 30
days of delivery. A 15% restocking fee applies to opened smart lock and
thermostat returns; smart plugs may be returned opened at no restocking
fee within the same window.

**5.2 Non-Returnable Items**

- Devices installed and then removed showing signs of installation
  damage (e.g., drilled mounting holes on a lock faceplate) beyond
  normal reversible installation.

- Products marked 'Final Sale' at time of purchase.

- Gift cards and extended warranty plans once activated.

**5.3 Return Shipping**

Contoso Electronics provides a prepaid return label for warranty
replacements. For non-warranty returns within the 30-day window, return
shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the return is due to a
shipping error or damaged-on-arrival item, in which case Contoso
Electronics covers the cost.

**6. Replacement & Repair Process**

Once an RMA is issued for an approved warranty claim:

1.  The customer ships the defective unit using the prepaid label within
    14 days of RMA issuance.

2.  Contoso Electronics inspects the returned unit upon receipt
    (typically 3–5 business days).

3.  If the defect is confirmed as covered, a replacement unit of the
    same model (or a comparable current model if discontinued) ships
    within 2 business days at no cost.

4.  If the defect is not confirmed as covered (e.g., found to be
    physical damage), the customer is notified and may choose to pay for
    repair, request the unit be returned unrepaired, or purchase a
    replacement at a discounted loyalty price.

Expedited replacement (advance shipment before the defective unit is
received) is available for smart locks only, due to home-security
impact, and requires a temporary credit-card hold as a deposit, released
once the original unit is received.

**7. Extended Warranty Plans**

Extended warranty plans ("Contoso Care") may be purchased within 60 days
of the original device purchase and extend coverage as shown in
Section 1. Contoso Care additionally covers:

- One accidental damage incident per device over the life of the plan
  (drops, spills).

- Priority shipping on all replacement units.

- Waived restocking fees on any non-warranty return within the plan
  period.

Contoso Care does not cover loss, theft, or intentional damage.

**8. Escalation Guidance for the AI Support Agent**

The Contoso Electronics Support Agent should transfer the conversation
to a live specialist rather than resolve the following itself:

- Any request to issue an RMA number or process a refund — these require
  a live specialist.

- Warranty claims involving ambiguous cause (unclear whether damage is a
  covered defect or an exclusion).

- Any customer expressing dissatisfaction with a prior warranty or
  return decision.

- Extended warranty (Contoso Care) purchases or cancellations.

The agent may, however, directly answer factual questions about coverage
windows, what is/is not covered, and general return-window policy using
this document.
